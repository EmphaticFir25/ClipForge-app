Plan questions

What counts toward my monthly minutes?

The length of each source file you upload. Clips you create don't add to it, and the total resets at the start of each month.

Can I switch plans later?

Plan changes will be handled through Stripe once payments open. Until then, your ClipForge team can change your plan for you.

Is the yearly price a discount?

Yes, yearly billing works out to 20% less than paying monthly for the same plan.
