Rights and takedown

Last updated: 2026-10-28

ClipForge is for footage you own or have permission to use. If you believe content on ClipForge infringes your rights, tell us and we will act on valid notices.

How to send a notice

Send a written notice to [designated agent email] with:

  1. Your name and contact details.
  2. The work you say is infringed, and where it appears (for example, the source footage or clip link, and the time range).
  3. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by you, your agent, or the law.
  4. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for one.
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

What we do

We review the notice and, where it is valid, remove or disable the material and tell the account owner. We may restore material if the account owner sends a counter-notice that meets the same standard, and we will restore it after 10 to 14 business days unless you have filed a court action.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe rights may be closed.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed by mistake, send a counter-notice to the same address with your name and contact details, the material removed and where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed in error, and consent to the jurisdiction of the courts where you live.

Other concerns

To report content that is unlawful for reasons other than copyright, or to report a safety problem, write to [legal contact email].
