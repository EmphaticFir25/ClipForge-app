Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, your plan, and a record of each sign-in. Sign-in links and API keys are stored only as one-way hashes, so we cannot read them back.
  • Footage and clips. The recordings you upload, the clips we make from them, and the captions and edits you make.
  • Usage. Source minutes used each month, jobs and their status, and errors, so we can enforce your plan and fix problems.
  • Payments. Stripe handles card details. We store the Stripe customer and subscription IDs and your plan. We never see or store card numbers.

How we use it

To run the service: to find moments, cut and caption clips, enforce your plan, send sign-in links, process payments, and answer support requests. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your footage to train models.

Processing on our servers

Transcription and clip scoring run on infrastructure we operate. Your footage is processed for your account only. Rights holders are covered by the takedown process.

Retention

Clips and source files are deleted after your plan's retention period (7, 30 or 90 days). Account records are kept while your account exists and for a short period after, to meet legal and accounting duties.

Who we share with

  • Stripe, for payments.
  • Email delivery providers, to send sign-in links.
  • Platforms you connect, such as YouTube or TikTok, only when you ask us to post to them.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can download your clips, delete them, sign out of every session, or close your account. To request a copy of your data or its deletion, contact us below.

Cookies

The app keeps your session in this browser tab so you stay signed in while it is open. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Keys and sign-in links are stored only as hashes. Payment webhooks are checked against Stripe's signature before any plan change. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us if you think something is wrong.
