Features

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do I need to own the footage?

Yes. Every upload asks you to confirm that you own it or have permission to clip it, and that you accept the terms of service.

What is a source minute?

One minute of the stream you upload. A three-hour stream uses 180 minutes. The free plan includes 60 a month.

What happens when I reach my limit?

New uploads pause until your allowance resets at the start of the next month. Your existing clips stay in your account.

Can I pay yet?

Payments open soon. Until then, accounts are set up by the ClipForge team, and the free plan works straight away.
